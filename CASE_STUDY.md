# The problem

A nonprofit technology consultant drafted an AI agent skill called `charity-donor-outreach`. It is meant to help fundraising staff at the ASPCA (American Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals) generate personalized donor letters at scale, with consistent, reliable output that keeps working as the donor list grows.

The goal of this project:

- Assess the draft and identify where it falls short.
- Describe the improvements worth making, why, and what impact they would have.
- Rewrite the skill to implement them.
